# C.R.A.P

The most important design priciples in UI design

- contrast
- Repetition
- Alignment
- Proximity
1. Contrast
    - Organize your design, establish hierarchy, emphasizes the focal point, adds visual interest.
2. Repetition\
    - creates consistency, increases users learnability, reduces confusion.
    - it adds some sort of structure to your layout
3. Alignment
    - Organizes and groups elements, creates rhythem, brings order to chaos.
4. Proximity
    - Makes elements seems related, brings understanding and meaning.
