# Prime Factors

## 1. Key Definitions

- **Factor:** A number that divides another number evenly, with no remainder.
- **Prime Number:** A whole number greater than 1 that has exactly two factors: 1 and itself.
- **Prime Factor:** A factor of a number that is also a prime number.

## 2. How to Find Prime Factors (Prime Factorization)

The **factor tree method** is a visual way to find the prime factors of a number.

1. **Start with the number** you want to factor.
2. **Split it** into any two factors.
3. **Check each factor.** If a factor is not a prime number, split it again.
4. **Repeat the process** until all the "branches" of the tree end in a prime number.
5. **Collect all the primes.** The prime factors are all the prime numbers at the ends of the branches.

*Example for 36:*`36 -> 6 x 6 -> (2 x 3) x (2 x 3)`
The prime factors are `2, 2, 3, 3`.
