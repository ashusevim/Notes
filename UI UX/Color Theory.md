# Color Theory

- Saturation describes intensity of the color.
- increasing the saturation makes the color richer and darker, while reducing the saturation makes the color faded and lighter.
- When we say 'light blue' and 'dark red', we are describing the changes in saturation.
- Lightness, also known as tone or value, defines the perceived brightness of a color compared to pure white.
